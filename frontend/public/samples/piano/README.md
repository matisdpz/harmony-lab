# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Original recordings: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

These 30 files (one note every minor third, A0 to C8, 128 kbps MP3) are the subset published by the
Tone.js project at https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/. The sampler pitch-shifts them to fill
the notes in between. They are served locally so the app works offline.
